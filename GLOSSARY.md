## Cheater recall

A **cheater recall** is a planned lane reset that uses a wave crash to buy time: build a slow push, push the stacked wave fully into the enemy turret, then recall, shop and heal while the opponent clears it. The aim is to walk back as the wave pushes towards you, losing little or no farm or experience and avoiding the need to spend Teleport. You return with resources and an item advantage while the opponent still needs a reset. This is an established wave-management term, not cheating or an exploit. Terminology reference: [PHuffZone's explanation, published 27 August 2020](https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-top-lane-wave-management-guide-cheater-recall/) — https://mobalytics.gg/blog/lol-top-lane-wave-management-guide-cheater-recall/ . Its old timings and shopping lists are not current-patch recommendations.

### How Ramy uses it on Teemo

Ramy repeatedly plans an early cheater recall, sometimes explicitly around level three or a third-wave crash. His examples show why the purchase and health reset matter, and why the opponent's ability to push back determines whether the recall is affordable:

- **Plan the crash before leaving.** Against Gangplank, he describes crashing wave three and returning ready to fight. His Dark Seal, Refillable Potion and Doran's Ring purchase order belongs to that recorded game. - **Return with an advantage and manage the bounce.** In the Darius game, he explains returning with Dark Seal, being stronger and holding the wave near his tower; an enemy recall then risks losing a cannon wave. [Transcript, 02:30](transcripts/iZ-48pMef8g.md#p-3a4bcfed8db63bc19428e6313cc5f397f0eb49c90229b451c8dd0300e4042fe5) · Uploaded 2026-08-18 · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iZ-48pMef8g&t=150s
- **Health and the extra purchase can be the payoff.** Against Akali, he credits the cheater recall with returning at full health with an extra item, then notices that killing an extra caster affected the wave's direction. [Transcript, 02:30](transcripts/x9i6jBai9D8.md#p-503742a6c98b0379a5d34c04424674f58fbf165b8dd3ba7f8497bb4b1525e3b3) · Uploaded 2026-03-01 · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x9i6jBai9D8&t=150s

[Transcript, 02:00](transcripts/j4sSxOFuHGs.md#p-cd22dc6e44fde67b0279cd19ae46daa13ab88ac8d3f0a68503d52d114d171418) · Uploaded 2026-05-21 · https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=j4sSxOFuHGs&t=120s

**Patch context:** these are examples of the decision, not a universal instruction to recall on wave three. Whether it works depends on the actual crash, both champions' wave clear, health, jungle pressure, recall and return time, and the patch's minion and item systems. The historical item choices above have not been validated for the current patch.
