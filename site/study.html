Five starting questions

What should I aim for in the first three waves?

Decide whether you can deny experience, build a wave to crash, or receive it safely. Against Garen, Ramy describes poking as the wave builds. The crash gives that pressure a purpose.

{{ref:242342fb073c69c507a17e9f3fa0d3726ea0768f908cb5bcd4a97a44397657ae}}
How do I know when a trade is safe?

Check your blind, the opponent’s threatening cooldowns, the wave and who levels up next. Against Ambessa, Ramy calls out a trade he took while his blind was down, then spots a better opening.

{{ref:3a0b274af1a036965288fb3799341b8e762bdd8a8dc6a70aebbd1b9064dfbf2d}}
Should my first item help me duel or clear waves?

Ask whether the opponent will let you keep attacking. In the Kennen game, Ramy weighs duelling power and health against waveclear. Compare what each purchase lets him do in that recording. Item effects can change between patches.

{{ref:0dfa6ac9555d1c3822ffa0941e016e37c1fcf3de3ee06d7144a14cfc1b1b2c7b}}
How do I keep lane pressure without dying to a gank?

Before you extend, work out which route the jungler could take and how you would leave. Against Yone, Ramy uses Qiyana’s starting side to decide when to ward for her level-three gank.

{{ref:f6f3f08dd8b2b48c437565801a57067febc5c69367aceb46f621198c38054172}}
I won lane. Where should that lead go?

If the other laner can refuse a fight, look for something the lead can buy elsewhere. In the Kennen game, Ramy considers moving mid once a solo kill stops looking available.

{{ref:f8ce06c3996a5d7393c8b6056d053bf9adeac29e08c98176d369d4e2d8bc22a2}}

What would you do here?

Choose a line of play, then open the recording notes to compare your reasoning.

Darius has left lane

Your freeze forced Darius off the wave. He went mid and has gained little there. The lane is yours for a moment.

Your call
Compare with the recording

Ramy pushes, then calls it a mistake. The freeze had left Darius with few options. Pushing gives him a wave he can return to. The value of holding here depends on what Darius can accomplish elsewhere.

{{ref:e45dd12f5158cd3ab23f5469fc6a6d377e3f505348f5712de38a80a0a2d7bab8}}

Tristana is about to recall

You could leave base alongside her. One more Gromp would finish an item, and drake is coming up.

Your call
Compare with the recording

Listen to both passages. Ramy first wants to leave base with Tristana. Then he considers accepting a timing gap for the purchase and checks the drake timer. The commentary captures him weighing those options.

Your call
